04

Future

Atlanta, Georgia

Future, born Nayvadius DeMun Wilburn, is an American rapper, singer and producer from Atlanta.

He became one of the defining artists of modern trap through his melodic delivery, Auto-Tuned vocals and influential production style.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Nayvadius DeMun Wilburn
  • Genre: trap and Southern hip-hop
  • Notable projects include DS2 and Hndrxx

05

Drake

Toronto, Canada

Drake, born Aubrey Drake Graham, is a Canadian rapper, singer and songwriter.

His music combines rap, R&B and pop influences.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Aubrey Drake Graham
  • Born in Toronto
  • Genres include hip-hop and R&B
