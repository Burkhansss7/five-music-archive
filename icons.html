Future

Atlanta

Future helped define the sound of modern Atlanta trap through melodic vocals and atmospheric production.

His influence reaches far beyond trap, shaping the vocal and production choices of younger generations.

05

Drake

Toronto

Drake combines rap, R&B, melody and pop structures into a globally recognizable sound.

His ability to move between genres made him one of the defining mainstream artists of his generation.
