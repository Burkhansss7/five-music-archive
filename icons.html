02 / ICONS

Artists who became bigger than a genre.

Future and Drake represent two different approaches to modern mainstream hip-hop.

04

Future

Atlanta, Georgia

Future, born Nayvadius DeMun Wilburn, is an American rapper, singer and producer from Atlanta. He became one of the defining artists of modern trap through his melodic delivery, Auto-Tuned vocals and highly influential production style.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Nayvadius DeMun Wilburn
  • Genre: trap and Southern hip-hop
  • Notable projects include DS2 and Hndrxx

05

Drake

Toronto, Canada

Drake, born Aubrey Drake Graham, is a Canadian rapper, singer and songwriter. Before becoming a musician, he was known for acting in the television series Degrassi: The Next Generation. His music mixes rap, R&B and pop influences.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Aubrey Drake Graham
  • Born in Toronto
  • Genres include hip-hop and R&B
