01 / NEW WAVE

Artists shaping a newer sound.

Three artists connected to the newer generation of experimental trap and rage music.

01

Yeat

Irvine, California

Yeat, born Noah Olivier Smith, is an American rapper, songwriter and producer. He became widely known in 2021 with projects including 4L and Up 2 Më. His music is recognized for unusual vocal delivery, heavy production and his own vocabulary.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Noah Olivier Smith
  • Genre: rage, trap, experimental hip-hop
  • Breakthrough period: 2021

02

Playboi Carti

Atlanta, Georgia

Playboi Carti, born Jordan Terrell Carter, is an Atlanta rapper known for his unconventional vocal style, minimalist lyrics and strong visual identity. His projects such as Die Lit and Whole Lotta Red became highly influential in modern trap and rage music.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Jordan Terrell Carter
  • Founder of the Opium label
  • Known for experimental vocal styles

03

Ken Carson

Atlanta, Georgia

Ken Carson is an Atlanta rapper and producer associated with Playboi Carti's Opium label. He started producing music at a young age and developed a loud electronic style built around distorted synths and energetic beats.

Quick facts

  • Born in Atlanta
  • Signed to Opium in 2019
  • Projects include Project X and A Great Chaos
