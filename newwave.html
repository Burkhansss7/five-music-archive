Artists shaping
a newer sound.

Experimental production, distorted textures and a new visual language inside modern hip-hop.

01

Yeat

United States

Yeat's music combines distorted production, unconventional vocals, unusual terminology and futuristic synth textures.

His work represents one of the most recognizable directions within contemporary experimental trap.

02

Playboi Carti

Atlanta

Carti built an identity around minimal lyrics, experimental vocals and a strong visual aesthetic.

His influence can be heard across a generation of artists exploring rage and alternative trap.

03

Ken Carson

Atlanta

Ken Carson combines aggressive electronic production with melodic, energetic performances.

His sound sits directly inside the newer generation shaped by rage production and internet culture.
