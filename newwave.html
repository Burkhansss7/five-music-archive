01

Yeat

Irvine, California

Yeat, born Noah Olivier Smith, is an American rapper, songwriter and producer. He became widely known in 2021 with projects including 4L and Up 2 Më.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Noah Olivier Smith
  • Genre: rage, trap, experimental hip-hop
  • Breakthrough period: 2021

02

Playboi Carti

Atlanta, Georgia

Playboi Carti, born Jordan Terrell Carter, is an Atlanta rapper known for his unconventional vocal style, minimalist lyrics and strong visual identity.

Quick facts

  • Real name: Jordan Terrell Carter
  • Founder of the Opium label
  • Known for experimental vocal styles

03

Ken Carson

Atlanta, Georgia

Ken Carson is an Atlanta rapper and producer associated with Playboi Carti's Opium label. He developed an energetic style built around distorted synths and heavy production.

Quick facts

  • Born in Atlanta
  • Signed to Opium
  • Projects include Project X and A Great Chaos
