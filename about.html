ABOUT THE PROJECT

Made by two
music listeners.

FIVE. is a responsive Web Technologies project focused on modern hip-hop artists.

THE IDEA

A personal archive, not a ranking.

The website introduces five artists and demonstrates how different styles of modern hip-hop can exist within one visual archive.
