ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Music remembered
through details.

FIVE. is a visual music archive focused on sound, identity, visuals and influence inside modern hip-hop.

THE IDEA

A personal archive, not a ranking.

FIVE. does not try to decide which artist is better.

Instead, the archive explores five artists who represent different sounds, aesthetics and moments in modern hip-hop.

Our goal is to combine music, visual identity and interactive web technologies in one project.

OUR APPROACH

Three things define FIVE.

01

Sound

Production styles, vocal techniques and sonic details that create a recognizable identity.

02

Visuals

Album artwork, photography, fashion and visual direction surrounding music.

03

Influence

How ideas move between artists, generations and different parts of modern hip-hop culture.
